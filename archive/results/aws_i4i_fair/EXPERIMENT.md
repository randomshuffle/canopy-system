# WHIR commitment scheduling audit

Investigated concurrent WHIR commitment scheduling after finding different eta scaling between the Merkle and Canopy harnesses. Six follow-up WHIR timing points completed before the run was stopped; the final reporting policy scales commitments from the same-scheme eta=1 measurement.

Original location: `results/aws_i4i_fair/`. Partial diagnostic follow-up; these samples are not mixed into the final campaign.

The retained logs, manifests, commands, and source bundles describe the original run; historical paths and source hashes have not been rewritten. See [final results and reproduction guidance](../../../results/README.md) for the current experiments.
