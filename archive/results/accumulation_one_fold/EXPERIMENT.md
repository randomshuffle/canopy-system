# Local one-fold accumulation comparison

Compared a plain same-code Merkle accumulator with a Canopy accumulator that reduces the old RS0 claim by one WHIR fold and batches the resulting RS1 claims. Local Apple M2 runs retain raw samples, optimized controls, proof/hash accounting, and an early reencoded-output variant.

Original location: `results/accumulation_one_fold/`. A separate accumulation study, with local timing conditions and an accumulator rather than the final paper workload.

The retained logs, manifests, commands, and source bundles describe the original run; historical paths and source hashes have not been rewritten. See [final results and reproduction guidance](../../../results/README.md) for the current experiments.
