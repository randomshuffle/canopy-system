# Local parallel prover optimization controls

Compared before/after GF(2^256) prover implementations at N=2^22, eta=1 on Apple M2 with eight workers. It evaluates parallel Bolt gather/dot/cross work and additive-FFT task thresholds, retaining an earlier fine-grained version.

Original location: `results/parallel_prover_n22_eta1/`. A local implementation study, not the final Intel campaign or evidence of a general WHIR speedup.

The retained logs, manifests, commands, and source bundles describe the original run; historical paths and source hashes have not been rewritten. See [final results and reproduction guidance](../../../results/README.md) for the current experiments.
