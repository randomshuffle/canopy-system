# Original AWS campaign and stopped continuation

Ran the first five-configuration i4i campaign using 96 workers, then resumed on persistent disk after the initial temporary build disappeared. The campaign stopped with 55 completed points; interrupted work is excluded. Pilot estimates, accepted samples, source bundles, and status records are preserved.

Original location: `results/aws_i4i/`. Superseded by the completed 32-worker campaign retained under results/aws_i4i_optimized/.

The retained logs, manifests, commands, and source bundles describe the original run; historical paths and source hashes have not been rewritten. See [final results and reproduction guidance](../../../results/README.md) for the current experiments.
