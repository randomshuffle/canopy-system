# WHIR half-length Canopy codeword study

Halved the Canopy RS block from 2N to N while retaining a three-variable initial fold and h=4. It compares proofs/hashes across seven sizes and local eta=1 timings against Merkle baselines and a full-length control.

Original location: `results/whir_half_block_eta1/`. A configuration study that informed the final half-length WHIR setup; local controls remain separate from the final AWS timings.

The retained logs, manifests, commands, and source bundles describe the original run; historical paths and source hashes have not been rewritten. See [final results and reproduction guidance](../../../results/README.md) for the current experiments.
